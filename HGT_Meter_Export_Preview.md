## Distribution Status Classification
- **No Distribution**: 0 units
- **Low Distribution**: < 2,000 units  
- **Moderate Distribution**: 2,000 - 4,999 units
- **High Distribution**: 5,000 - 9,999 units
- **Very High Distribution**: ≥ 10,000 units

## Key Insights
- **Free State dominates** with 58.9% of all HGT meter distribution
- **Top 2 provinces** (Free State + Gauteng) account for nearly 75% of distribution
- **Eastern Cape** has no meter distribution
- **5 out of 6 provinces** have active distribution
- **Average distribution** is 8,929 units per active province
